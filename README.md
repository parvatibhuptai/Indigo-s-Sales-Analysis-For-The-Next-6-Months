# IndiGo Sales Analysis For The Next Six Months

This project is a comprehensive Excel-based financial and sales analysis predicting the revenue, cost, and profitability of IndiGo Airlines' newly launched international and long-haul routes over a six-month horizon. 

## Team & My Role
This was a collaborative academic project developed alongside Asmita Baul, Dhruv Arora, and Sujan Shrestha for a Spreadsheet Modelling course. 
* **My Contribution:** I was responsible for gathering accurate financial and operational data from IndiGo's annual reports, structuring the underlying analytical logic, and assisting with the core Excel spreadsheet modeling and formulas, and drafting the final financial interpretations.
* **Team Contributions:** Other members collaborated on data cleaning and data visualization.

## Project Objective
* Collect and organize route-level financial data, including aircraft models, flight frequencies, and capacity constraints.
* Predict sales growth and profitability driven by the airline's expansion into new long-haul international flights.
* Apply advanced Excel functions and data visualization tools to interpret real-world airline economics.

## Methodology & Tech Stack
* **Core File:** All analysis is contained within `IndiGo_Route_Financials.xlsx`.
* **Data Gathering:** We manually extracted operational metrics (fleet size, fuel costs, premium seat pricing) from IndiGo’s FY 2022-25 Annual Reports and investor presentations.
* **Excel Modeling:** We calculated estimated revenues and operating costs using seat configurations, estimated load factors, and flight duration categories.
* **Analytical Tools:** Heavily utilized Pivot Tables, conditional formatting, and logical functions (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `IF`, `COUNTIF`) to segment route profitability by aircraft type.

##  Key Business Insights
* **Overall Route Profitability:** Across 23 new international routes, the total estimated annual revenue is ₹2425.36 Cr against operating costs of ₹3303.94 Cr, indicating a short-term structural gap and net loss of ~₹878.57 Cr during the expansion phase.
* **Wide-Body vs. Narrow-Body:** Long-range aircraft like the Boeing 787-9 (Leased) and Airbus A321XLR heavily outperform the A320 fleet in profitability (e.g., Mumbai-London yielding a +3.3% margin).
* **Economy vs. Premium Yield:** Economy class dominates at ~78% of total revenue (₹1711.95 Cr); however, "Stretch" (Business Class) revenue accounts for ~22% and acts as the critical driver for making long-haul routes financially viable.
* **The Medium-Haul Struggle:** Several A320-operated medium-haul routes (e.g., Mumbai-Bali, Delhi-Hanoi) recorded significant losses, suggesting a need for pricing adjustments or demand stimulation.
